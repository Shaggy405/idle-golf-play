# Idle Golf

A golf career that plays itself. The golfer plays hole after hole on their own; you decide the contracts, sponsors, equipment and tournaments.

**Play it now: https://shaggy405.github.io/idle-golf-play/**

Or download it and play offline: get `idle-golf.html` from the [latest release](https://github.com/Shaggy405/idle-golf-play/releases/latest) and double-click it. It is one file, it installs nothing, and it does not go online.

Your progress is saved in the browser you play in. "Export save" and "Import save" at the foot of the page carry a game from one place to another, for instance from the website to the downloaded file.

## Found a problem, or have an idea?

Press **Send feedback** at the foot of the game. It opens a new report here, already filled in with where your game stood (the hole, your golfer's levels, the build), so all you need to add is what happened and what you expected. Then press "Create" (called "Submit new issue" on some screens). You need a free GitHub account to send it.

You can also write one from scratch on the [Issues](https://github.com/Shaggy405/idle-golf-play/issues) page. If something went badly wrong, "Export save" gives you a file you can attach, which lets the problem be seen exactly as you had it.

The build you are playing is shown at the foot of the page.
